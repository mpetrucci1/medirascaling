Questions

The things people ask on the first call.

What actually is an AI operating system?

A database of how your business communicates, plus the automations that read from it and write back into it. It is not a chatbot bolted onto your site. It sits behind your inbox, your outreach and your reporting, and it is the same brain in all three.

Will it really sound like me?

That is the part we spend the most time on. We train it on your own sent mail and your real proposals, then you correct the first fifty drafts. After that people stop being able to tell, because there is nothing to tell. The words are yours, sorted and reused.

What do you need from me to build it?

Access to your mailbox, a folder of past proposals, and about two hours of your time in week one. After the build we need roughly fifteen minutes a day from you for the first fortnight, spent approving drafts. That approval is the training.

How long until it is running?

One system is one to three weeks depending on which one. We build in that order on purpose, so you have something working before you have spent much. Nothing here takes a quarter.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

You catch it at approval, because nothing sends without you. You correct the draft, and the correction goes back into the database, so that class of mistake stops happening. Being wrong is how it gets right.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

No. It sits underneath what you have. If your team lives in a particular inbox, CRM or scheduler, the system reads and writes there rather than asking anyone to move.
