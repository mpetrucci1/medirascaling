## Format, time, price, venue, attendance

### Takeaway
Cobb Connections is a Chamber-run networking event, described as monthly, on Monday afternoons 3-4:30 p.m. at the Chamber office, with table discussions. It is complimentary for members with pre-registration. I found no confirmed non-member price (the $25 figure is not supported; one third-party listing shows $250). No attendance numbers found.

### Cited Findings
- Official event page reportedly says the event is held monthly on Monday afternoons, 3-4:30 p.m., at the Cobb Chamber; admission is complimentary with membership and pre-registration is required — [Cobb Chamber event page](https://cobbchamber.org/event/cobb-connections/) (via search summary, not fetched)
- Chamber membership levels list "complimentary admission for you and/or your employees to Cobb Connections, Mastermind Book Club and Business After Hours" — [Membership Levels](https://cobbchamber.org/member-center/become-a-member/membership-levels/) (via search summary)
- Chamber's older Networking page reportedly says the event runs "one/two Mondays per month" 3-4:30 p.m., which conflicts with "once a month" on the event page — [Networking page](https://www.cobbchamber.org/Member-Services/Networking.aspx) (via search summary; page may be outdated legacy site)
- A third-party listing for a "July 13" session shows cost $250; unclear whether member/non-member or even the same product; no year confirmed (July 13 is a Monday in 2026) — [Highlight Georgia](https://highlightgeorgia.com/event/cobb-connections/) (unverified aggregator)
- Walk-ins accepted first-come; online registration closes ~3 days before or at capacity (July example: closes July 10) — search summary of event listing, [Cobb Chamber event page](https://cobbchamber.org/event/cobb-connections/)
- Venue: Chamber office, 1100 Circle 75 Parkway, Suite 1000, Atlanta (Cobb), GA 30339 — [MDJ Business Brief Case, Oct 2025](https://www.mdjonline.com/101125-mdj-business-brief-case/article_1cb5a2ac-cd3a-49de-ab93-4bb987ddd293.html) and [Pope & Land](https://popeandland.com/cobb-chamber-announces-move-to-new-location/) (via summaries)
- Format: sponsor recaps say attendees engaged in table discussions, exchanged perspectives, and laid groundwork for collaborations — [Manay CPA](https://www.manaycpa.com/manay-cpa-supports-and-engages-in-cobb-chambers-cobb-connections-networking-event/) (promotional)
- Chamber sponsorship manuals list Cobb Connections as a recurring program with sponsorship tiers (Manay was a "Yearlong Networking Sponsor") — [2024 manual](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Sponsorship-Manual-2024.pdf), [2026 manual](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Sponsorship-Manual_reduced-size.pdf). A search summary said the 2026 manual shows "Chamber Connections" in August; unclear if same event.

### Inferences
- Member cost is effectively $0; the "$25" and "member vs non-member" structure is unconfirmed. The $250 may be a different fee type or an error.
- Whether it is speed networking vs open mixer is unresolved: sources only say "table discussions".

### Gaps
