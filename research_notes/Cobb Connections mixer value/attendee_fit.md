# Cobb Chamber / Cobb Connections: attendee composition and fit for a B2B AI agency selling to clinical research / healthcare

Research date: 2026-10-16. Caveat: WebFetch failed (DNS errors for cobbchamber.org and manaycpa.com), so all findings are from search-result snippets, not full-page reads. The member directory, event pages and PDFs were not opened. Treat details as unverified until checked.

## What types of businesses/roles attend Cobb Connections and Chamber mixers?

### Takeaway
Cobb Connections is a monthly (possibly bi-monthly, schedule unconfirmed) table-format networking event. The only attendee evidence is a CPA sponsor's press release plus the Chamber's own descriptions, which point to small business owners and professional services. No attendee roster or breakdown was found.

### Cited Findings
- Cobb Connections is described as a monthly networking event run by the Cobb Chamber, with table discussions among local business owners and professionals — [Manay CPA](https://www.manaycpa.com/manay-cpa-supports-and-engages-in-cobb-chambers-cobb-connections-networking-event/) (sponsor's own press release, 2025)
- Manay CPA was a "Yearlong Networking Sponsor"; the release says the event brings together entrepreneurs, business leaders, and community stakeholders — [Manay CPA](https://www.manaycpa.com/manay-cpa-supports-and-engages-in-cobb-chambers-cobb-connections-networking-event/)
- A third-party listing gives the next event as Oct 19, 2026 at the Cobb Chamber; not confirmed on the Chamber site — [Shared Audiences listing](https://www.sharedaudiences.com/events/cobb-connections-2026) (low-reliability aggregator)
- A 2022 Chamber sponsorship manual lists Cobb Connections as bi-monthly; the 2024 and 2026 manuals reportedly no longer list it under that name — [2022 manual](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/2022-Sponsorship-Manual_REDUCED-2.pdf), [2026 manual](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Sponsorship-Manual_reduced-size.pdf) (conflicts with the monthly description; this comes from a search summary, not a page read)
- Business After Hours runs six times a year, hosted at member venues such as Comcast (Aug 12, 2026), Glory Haus, LOUD Security Systems and Georgia Highlands College — [Cobb County Courier](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2026/07/cobb-chamber-to-host-business-after-hours-networking-event-at-comcast-cable-communications-aug-12/), [Cobb Chamber](https://cobbchamber.org/cobb-chamber-announces-business-after-hours-schedule-for-the-fall/) (fall schedule year unconfirmed)
- Other Chamber events: Cobb Young Professionals, Marquee Monday luncheons (Children's Healthcare of Atlanta CEO spoke in Dec 2024), First Monday Breakfast, SelectCobb entrepreneurship/small business series — [Courier](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2024/12/childrens-healthcare-of-atlanta-ceo-to-speak-at-next-cobb-chamber-marquee-monday/), [Chamber](https://cobbchamber.org/selectcobb-entrepreneurship-small-business-series-continues-with-networking-program-on-feb-17/)
- The 84th Annual Dinner (Feb 28, 2026) drew over 1,200 business and community leaders; Wellstar presented it — [Cobb County Courier via search summary](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2023/01/cobb-chamber-celebrates-2022-at-its-81st-annual-dinner/) (the 2026 dinner figure came from the search summary; the linked page is the 2023 dinner story, so verify)

### Inferences
- Professional-services sellers (CPAs, as in the Manay example) clearly attend as sponsors. By extension, insurance, real estate, financial services and corporate reps are likely present, but no source confirms this mix. That is an assumption.
- The Cobb Connections audience is probably mostly local SMB owners and service providers, i.e. sellers and peers, not clinical-research buyers.

### Gaps
- No attendee counts, roster, or job-title breakdown for Cobb Connections.
- Current schedule, pricing, and member vs. non-member access not confirmed.
- Chamber site pages could not be fetched.

## Chamber size and healthcare/life sciences footprint

### Takeaway
The Chamber has about 2,600 member organizations (mostly small businesses). Healthcare is present through Wellstar (a major sponsor) and a SelectCobb healthcare/biotech target sector. There is a Healthcare Industry Council event series, but its current activity is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- About 2,600 member organizations, mostly small businesses, representing 18,000+ business leaders (2026 agenda); the 2025 agenda cited about 2,700 — [Cobb Chamber state legislative agenda](https://cobbchamber.org/advocacy/state-legislative-agenda) (via search summary)
- Wellstar presented the 84th Annual Dinner; Wellstar Cobb Hospital's president spoke at a South Cobb Area Council meeting in 2022 — [Cobb County Courier](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2022/06/wellstar-cobb-hospital-president-to-report-on-the-state-of-healthcare-in-south-cobb/)
- Wellstar Health System is in the Chamber's member directory (Hospitals category) and has a Leadership Cobb overview deck — [directory](https://web.cobbchamber.org/Hospitals/Wellstar-Health-System-24404), [PDF](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Wellstar-Overview_LeadershipCobb.pdf)
- Feb 2025 Chairman's Circle list includes health-related names (A.G. Rhodes Health & Rehab, Good Samaritan Health Center, Cobb & Douglas Public Health, Children's Healthcare of Atlanta) — [Chairman's Circle PDF](https://cobbchamber.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Chairmans-Circle-Member-List-02.03.25.pdf) (search summary; list was only partly visible)
- The Cobb Chamber and SelectCobb hosted a Healthcare Industry Council meeting (Sept 8, 2022, employee wellness topic) — [Courier](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2022/08/the-cobb-chamber-and-selectcobb-to-host-healthcare-industry-council-meeting/), [Chamber](https://cobbchamber.org/selectcobb-to-host-healthcare-industry-council-on-sept-8/)
- SelectCobb target industries include healthcare and biotechnology. It names Osmotica Pharmaceuticals, MyMedics and Quintiles as sector players, and says most are under 10 years old with 50 or fewer employees (an older release, date unknown) — [SelectCobb target industries](https://selectcobb.com/site-selection/target-industries/), [Osmotica release](https://selectcobb.com/?p=3006)
- Fresenius Medical planned to add 120 jobs in Cobb (date not seen) — [Saporta Report](https://saportareport.com/fresenius-medical-plans-to-add-120-jobs-in-cobb-county/sections/reports/maria_saporta/)

### Inferences
- Healthcare presence is mainly providers (hospital systems, rehab, public health), not research or sponsor companies. This is weak evidence from partial lists.
- The Healthcare Industry Council is an economic-development convening that may suit relationship-building with Wellstar and similar providers, though it is not a clinical research venue.

### Gaps
- No member count for healthcare or life sciences specifically.
- No confirmation that the Healthcare Industry Council is active in 2026.
- Medical device firms in Cobb: nothing found.
- Chairman's Circle list only partly seen.

### Takeaway
No confirmed CRO or research-site member was found. That is an absence of evidence only, since the directory was not searchable here.

- No Cobb site-by-site verification was done, and Georgia Bio and Atlanta clinical research directories were not examined.

## Evidence of attendees landing clients or deals

### Takeaway
None found. The only public evidence is sponsor-authored press describing networking, not closed business.

### Cited Findings
- Manay CPA's release quotes the event as "invaluable for meeting local businesses and hearing their future goals directly"; no client win is claimed — [Manay CPA](https://www.manaycpa.com/manay-cpa-connected-with-local-businesses-at-cobb-connections/)
- The Chamber claims it hosts hundreds of events a year to create connections and "potential business" (promotional claim, no figures) — [Cobb Chamber](https://cobbchamber.org/?p=31239)

### Inferences
- Value is likely relationship and referral based and indirect. A realistic fit for the agency is meeting SMB practices and providers, such as Wellstar-adjacent vendors, physician groups and dental or medical practices, rather than CRO or pharma decision-makers.
